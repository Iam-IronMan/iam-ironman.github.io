Hi, I'm Jiahang. I work on data and algorithms for 3D generation. My work with ByteDance Seed3D spans geometry, textures, part-level assets, and scenes—from constructing and curating training data to building the systems that deliver it.

I received my master's degree from Zhejiang University, where I worked on facial animation using differentiable rendering. I'm interested in how graphics knowledge, data, and learning come together to create better 3D content.

03 / PRACTICE

Making training data work

Alongside model researchers, I turn data requirements into practical algorithms and repeatable pipelines.

01

Data quality & curation

VLM-based quality assessment, canonical orientation, and visual deduplication. My TextureVLM work covered quality standards, label alignment, and data filtering; the resulting data was used and validated in model training.

VLM assessment · Orientation · Deduplication
02

Data construction through engines, people & agents

I build data-production workflows with Unreal Engine, crowdsourcing, and agents. This includes agent-assisted creation of lettering, typography, and textures for 3D assets.

Unreal Engine→Scene collision data
Crowdsourcing→Geometry & texture data
Agents→Lettering & texture data

I also work with model teams to connect geometry and texture processing, understand remeshing and voxel representations, and address data problems revealed by model outputs.

03

Infrastructure & retrieval

3D asset management, metadata and data-access interfaces, distributed processing, and scene-asset retrieval. I built systems around MongoDB and vector search to make curated assets easier to find and use.

Asset libraries · Data pipelines · Vector search
